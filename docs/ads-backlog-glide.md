# ADS backlog glide path

Read-only extension of Necessidade ADS. `GET /api/staff-coverage/ads/backlog-glide?horizonDays=14` accepts 7 or 14 calendar days including today. It requires the current active database user's existing ADS planning, Performance and Real Time queue permissions. Both successful and failed responses are private/no-store. No migration, operational write, stored requirement refresh, notification or delivery job is introduced. The incoming-volume source is canonical version `volume-v6-ads-daily-reference-online`; its historical reference is fixed per Brazil day while the operational estimate still follows eligible Real Time cycles. The ABS and capacity rules below are preserved.

The projection starts at the newest operational Real Time queue cycle, not the browser's current hour. Backlog is the sum of ADS queue backlog in that cycle; snapshots are not summed. A late reimport cannot move the selected cycle backwards. Import age and operational cycle age are both exposed. At more than 90 minutes old, or with a source clock more than five minutes ahead, the curve remains tied to its source and the clearance ETA is suppressed. A cycle from a previous calendar day is reported stale without inventing the missing elapsed trajectory.

Hourly recurrence is `max(0, previous backlog + canonical future arrivals in interval - effective capacity in interval)`. The first interval begins at the actual snapshot minute. Unadjusted hourly forecast amounts are prorated to that interval; revised current-day points use `forecastRemainingInInterval`, which integrates only `remainingInput` after `observedUntil`. The observed part of the hour is already represented in live backlog and must not be added again. Capacity uses saved effective schedule windows and individual submits per scheduled hour from the existing ADS planning reference. It includes a preceding-day shift that continues past midnight, deduplicates overlapping minutes per employee, and never discounts breaks or applies the old 1.29 headcount factor again. A preceding-night employee without an individual planning reference uses an already-exposed canonical skill fallback, or remains explicitly unavailable; a partial donor pool is never reconstructed from future roster members.

Expected ABS comes from the preceding seven complete operational calendar days, using the canonical absent-days / scheduled-days status classifiers at full precision. The read includes current ADS agent profiles only when their saved schedule slot belongs to ADS (or the slot has no saved LOB and uses the legacy current-registry fallback); PROJECT slots are excluded. Historical days are not discarded retroactively because of a current employee leave, termination or deletion. Job-title aliases use the existing classifier, after aggregation without personal rows. The source requires schedule records on all seven days, a positive eligible denominator and no remaining ESCALADO statuses; otherwise ABS is unavailable, with no fixed 7.5% fallback. An observed zero is valid when coverage and denominator are complete.

ABS reduces capacity only for shifts whose presence is not yet confirmed. A current shift with PRESENTE/ATRASO has no second ABS discount. AttendanceRecord can exclude a confirmed absence even when the schedule still says ESCALADO. Confirmed early departure excludes the remaining current shift. Presence is shift-specific and cannot imply attendance in a later shift; scheduled end times always cap capacity. Schedule statuses already excluded by the coverage adapter are not discounted again.

Missing productivity is distinct from observed zero. Missing or duplicate forecast hour keys, invalid schedules, or unavailable backlog cannot become zero or produce an ETA. No clearance inside the horizon returns a null ETA; there is no extrapolation. The response reports the first projected zero at hourly resolution and separately flags subsequent reaccumulation. Missing information anywhere in the horizon conservatively suppresses the ETA, including a first zero before a missing hour.

The RT reader also reports current snapshot integrity separately from intraday-history availability. If the final cycle loses a previously observed ADS queue, contains a duplicate/invalid counter, or is a newer global queue import without ADS, the glide withholds the partial backlog sum and its clearance ETA. A missing older interval with a valid recovered final snapshot can disable the online revision without falsely declaring today's backlog incomplete; the historical forecast remains the fallback in that case.

This is a consolidated ADS capacity scenario. It assumes capacity can be shared across queues and cannot guarantee that every queue is empty: skill, priority and routing constraints are not represented as a queue allocation model.

The canonical ADS forecast now separates a robust historical prior from a current-day revision. The prior uses weekday medians and the latest seven complete calendar days, with a weekday-specific hourly profile, for every weekday. It is not a fixed 11,000/day assumption. Eligible current-day RT arrivals revise only the unelapsed hours of today; subsequent days keep the historical prior. `baselinePoints` remain available as the original reference. This is a deterministic online revision, not retraining the historical model or feeding forecasts back as observations. See `docs/volume-forecast.md` for the exact ratios and historical eligibility requirements.

The new aggregate-only forecast reader runs after the historical cache. It selects the last successful global queue batch per cycle before filtering authoritative ADS queue IDs, so a newer batch with no ADS cannot revive an older ADS frame. The union of queue inventories must be present in every selected frame, with a 00:00 baseline, the ending cycle and gaps no larger than 30 minutes. It computes counter changes per queue before aggregation and returns no raw queue/person details. A missing frame, counter inconsistency or stale source leaves the prior in place with an availability warning, rather than inventing zero demand. Cycle/import freshness is limited to 90 minutes; future source times beyond five minutes cannot drive the revision. The optional internal `asOfCycle` aligns the revision with the selected backlog cycle. These source checks are separate from the backlog-age checks that suppress the glide's clearance ETA.

The historical prior may be cached for 24 hours, but RT intervals and today's revision are read after that cache on every canonical request. The returned forecast version incorporates the RT fingerprint and method version. New eligible RT data therefore need no new Performance import or cache expiry to change today's remainder. No separate forecast is fitted by the glide or the browser.

A known positive restart after an all-zero prior is a separate availability guard. The prior remains inspectable, but canonical operational points for today and later dates are withheld; the resulting missing-forecast state suppresses clearance ETA. It must not project a zero-demand future merely because the old reference was zero.

The demand context exposes the original day reference, actual cumulative arrivals since midnight, expected arrivals over that same elapsed interval, the revised remaining forecast after the selected cycle, and actual plus revised remaining forecast. Its `adjusted` flag identifies an active canonical online revision. The updated day estimate is conditional on the observed cycle and remaining-demand method; it is not a clearance guarantee. Comparisons of expected arrivals use the frozen historical reference, not already-revised elapsed points. Actual arrivals are calculated from per-queue cumulative-counter deltas with reset handling; they require a midnight baseline, an ending cycle and continuous half-hour coverage for every selected ADS queue. Missing boundaries, invalid counters or conflicting duplicate cycles return unavailable rather than zero. The independent recent-three-hour comparison uses the same observation rules and can remain available when the daily midnight baseline is missing. Daily forecast values require all 24 unique valid hourly points; fractional remaining intervals are integrated without rounding.

KAP's upstream day resets at midnight in China, 13:00 São Paulo, as already documented in `docs/ads-productivity-alerts.md` and the operational-shift counter calculation in `ads-online-productivity-report-core.ts`. At that boundary the new counter is the interval contribution even when it does not decrease (for example, an old counter of 1 and a new counter of 2 contribute 2). Any decrease outside the known reset makes the intersecting observed window unavailable. Civil midnight is only the baseline of the Brazilian reporting day; it is not another counter reset. The source check found this 13:00 reset in all five available exact 12:30/13:00 checkpoint pairs from 9–15 October 2026, with 84 queue baselines each; on 15 October a drop-only rule missed one arrival in a small queue.

`points[].timestamp`, `intervalStart`, and `clearanceAt` are operational São Paulo wall clock encoded in UTC, consistent with the imported volume and schedule fields. Format their UTC clock components without subtracting the São Paulo offset a second time. `calculatedAt` and `sources.snapshotImportedAt` are genuine UTC instants. `sources.latestProductionAt` and `latestVolumeAt` retain the imported wall-clock convention.

The visible glide panel refreshes hourly, when the tab becomes visible again and on manual recalculation. Freshness labels also advance between requests; a retained curve after a failed refresh remains inspectable but does not present its old ETA as current. This does not schedule a background import, message or delivery. The Performance Forecast tab follows the same hourly visible-tab refresh policy.

Forecast accuracy shown in Performance evaluates the historical base on completed historical targets. It does not measure the accuracy of the intraday revision or this clearance scenario. RT history is retained for at least seven days and can contain gaps; correctness tests are not an independent online accuracy benchmark. ABS, productivity uncertainty, source age and future arrivals remain distinct reasons for a conditional or unavailable ETA.
