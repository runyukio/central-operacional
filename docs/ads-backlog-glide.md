# ADS backlog glide path

Read-only extension of Necessidade ADS. `GET /api/staff-coverage/ads/backlog-glide?horizonDays=14` accepts 7 or 14 calendar days including today. It requires the current active database user's existing ADS planning and Real Time queue permissions. Both successful and failed responses are private/no-store. No migration, operational write, stored requirement refresh, notification or delivery job is introduced.

The projection starts at the newest operational Real Time queue cycle, not the browser's current hour. Backlog is the sum of ADS queue backlog in that cycle; snapshots are not summed. A late reimport cannot move the selected cycle backwards. Import age and operational cycle age are both exposed. At more than 90 minutes old, or with a source clock more than five minutes ahead, the curve remains tied to its source and the clearance ETA is suppressed. A cycle from a previous calendar day is reported stale without inventing the missing elapsed trajectory.

Hourly recurrence is `max(0, previous backlog + canonical forecast in interval - effective capacity in interval)`. The first interval is prorated from the actual snapshot minute. Capacity uses saved effective schedule windows and individual submits per scheduled hour from the existing ADS planning reference. It includes a preceding-day shift that continues past midnight, deduplicates overlapping minutes per employee, and never discounts breaks or applies the old 1.29 headcount factor again. A preceding-night employee without an individual planning reference uses an already-exposed canonical skill fallback, or remains explicitly unavailable; a partial donor pool is never reconstructed from future roster members.

The expected ABS is fixed at 7.5%, reducing capacity only for shifts whose presence is not yet confirmed. A current shift with PRESENTE/ATRASO has no second ABS discount. AttendanceRecord can exclude a confirmed absence even when the schedule still says ESCALADO. Confirmed early departure excludes the remaining current shift. Presence is shift-specific and cannot imply attendance in a later shift; scheduled end times always cap capacity. Schedule statuses already excluded by the coverage adapter are not discounted again.

Missing productivity is distinct from observed zero. Missing or duplicate forecast hour keys, invalid schedules, or unavailable backlog cannot become zero or produce an ETA. No clearance inside the horizon returns a null ETA; there is no extrapolation. The response reports the first projected zero at hourly resolution and separately flags subsequent reaccumulation. Missing information anywhere in the horizon conservatively suppresses the ETA, including a first zero before a missing hour.

This is a consolidated ADS capacity scenario. It assumes capacity can be shared across queues and cannot guarantee that every queue is empty: skill, priority and routing constraints are not represented as a queue allocation model. The canonical incoming-volume forecast remains unchanged and does not refit on each Real Time import.

`points[].timestamp`, `intervalStart`, and `clearanceAt` are operational São Paulo wall clock encoded in UTC, consistent with the imported volume and schedule fields. Format their UTC clock components without subtracting the São Paulo offset a second time. `calculatedAt` and `sources.snapshotImportedAt` are genuine UTC instants. `sources.latestProductionAt` and `latestVolumeAt` retain the imported wall-clock convention.

Verification: `tsx --test src/lib/ads-backlog-glide-*.test.ts`, TypeScript validation, production build. Tests do not connect to the database or invoke cron/webhook routes.
